# Warnings and lights

**Made for fun, not tested on a FIP yet.** Testers welcome: if something's off, fix it in your fork and share it.

![Warnings and lights](../../previews/Warnings%20and%20lights.png)

2 gauges. **Download this set: [Warnings and lights.zip](../../downloads/Warnings%20and%20lights.zip)**, unzip it, and add the folder in SPAD.neXt (see the [front page](../../README.md)). Or get every set at once: [All gauges.zip](../../downloads/All%20gauges.zip).

| Gauge | | About it |
|---|---|---|
| **Lights switches** | <img src="../../previews/Warnings%20and%20lights/Lights%20switches.png" width="200" alt="Lights switches"> | Landing, taxi, nav, beacon, strobe and logo lights on S1-S6: press to switch, the button light shows when it's on. |
| **Warning lights** | <img src="../../previews/Warnings%20and%20lights/Warning%20lights.png" width="200" alt="Warning lights"> | Master warning and caution, parking brake, low fuel, oil pressure, vacuum, volts, low height and autopilot lights. S1/S2 acknowledge master warning/caution, S3 sets the parking brake; their button lights follow. |

Free and open source (AGPL-3.0), made from scratch. See the [front page](../../README.md).
